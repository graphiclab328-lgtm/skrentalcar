Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our rental requirements, security deposits, fuel policies, and options.

For Pakistani residents renting self-drive, you need: (a) Original CNIC, (b) Valid Pakistani Driving License, and (c) a copy of a utility bill or employment ID for address verification. For overseas Pakistanis and international visitors, a valid Passport, International Driving Permit (IDP) or home country license, and visa stamp are required. If renting with a professional chauffeur, only standard contact information and a CNIC/Passport copy are needed.

A fully refundable security deposit is collected upon vehicle delivery for self-drive rentals (amount depends on the vehicle class, ranging from PKR 15,000 for hatchbacks to PKR 50,000 for luxury SUVs). The deposit is 100% refunded immediately upon returning the car in safe condition with no traffic fines or damages. For rentals with chauffeur, zero security deposit is required.

We operate on a transparent Same-to-Same Fuel Policy. The vehicle is delivered to you with a documented fuel level (usually full or half tank). You simply return the vehicle with the equivalent fuel level. If you prefer, we can also provide pre-fueled service upon request.

Standard city rentals include a generous 200 km per day allowance. Extra kilometers are charged at modest nominal rates per kilometer depending on the car model. For long-distance intercity tours (e.g., Lahore to Islamabad, Northern Areas, Swat, Murree), customized unlimited or extended mileage packages can be arranged in advance.

We believe in stress-free travel. You can cancel or reschedule your reservation free of charge up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled pick-up time. There are no penalty deductions. Simply notify us via phone, WhatsApp, or through our 24/7 Concierge desk.
